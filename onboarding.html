Chrona

Chrona is a silent, beautiful companion designed to mirror your attention. It quietly reflects how you spend your time online so you can discover healthy focus habits.

✓

Zero External Sync

Every millisecond of tracked history remains entirely on your local machine. No logins, no tracking scripts, and no clouds.

✓

Clean Data Principle

We extract only the core website name (like github.com). Sub-pages, exact search queries, and passwords are never read.

✓

Silent & Calm

No arbitrary shaming scores, no modal blockades, and no continuous alerts. Chrona is silent until you open it.
